How to use it in Minecraft

  1. Open your Joy of Painting canvas and palette.
  2. Set the app's color slots to match your in-game palette.
  3. Set your brush to 1 pixel for the coordinate guide.
  4. Follow each step and mark the pixel when it is painted.
  5. Coordinates start at X=1 from the left and Y=1 from the bottom.

Tip: keep a small canvas such as 32×32. Detail preservation sharpens the original before reducing it, so important outlines and features survive without increasing your pixel count. The guide assumes a fully opaque 1-pixel brush.

4. Paint in Minecraft

No helper or extra downloads needed. Use the step-by-step guide above: choose the shown color, place the pixel at the shown coordinate, then click Mark placed and Next pixel. You can pause anytime and download the step list as a text file.

4. Automatic Minecraft painting

Download and run the small Windows launcher, then calibrate the canvas and each used palette color once. Keep Minecraft visible and do not move its window after calibration.
